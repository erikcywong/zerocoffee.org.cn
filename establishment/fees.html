Why the price is not a per-seat price

A software seat costs nothing to duplicate. Evidence costs person-days, review and liability every time. The day rate is ¥3,800 with a 25% overhead, and a first-year verification is six person-days — a number anyone can check against the fee.

Correction on the record: an earlier version of our method priced verification at ¥15,000 per site. That figure predates any accredited route and cannot support an ISO 14065-level engagement. The published schedule above replaced it.

Buyer-side schedule

A label is paid for twice: once by the supplier for the evidence, once by the buyer who needs it. The buyer-side price is set by evidence grade, and the signature share inside it is fixed, so a conversation about price is a conversation about the grade — not about the fee.

GradeMetered shareBuyer pays / batch Signature share (licensed partner)Kept by the evidence layer
A≥ 70%¥80¥40¥40
B≥ 45%¥60¥40¥20
C< 45%¥40¥40¥0

The ¥40 is what a licensed certification body is paid to countersign one batch. The remainder is the spread for whoever holds the buyer relationship — which is why this table is published rather than negotiated. Supplier-side fees above remain payable for the measurement and the register entry; nothing here is a commission on a conclusion, and no fee changes the grade.
