Establishment file · Conditions

Conditions

Six conditions, none optional. Missing one does not make the signature worthless — it makes it a liability for whoever relied on it.

1 · Legal form

The body must be an entity that can be sued, with stated liability limits and professional indemnity sized to the claims it makes. A partnership or a project of another company cannot carry the consequence of a wrong conclusion.

RequirementWhat we have
Registered entityCompany limited by guarantee, Hong Kong (in incorporation)
Liability capPublished in the engagement letter, per engagement
IndemnityTo be bound before the first paid engagement

2 · Governance

An impartiality committee that can veto commercial decisions, chaired by someone outside the industry being verified. Without it, a client who pays for a conclusion is one meeting away from getting it.

3 · Competence

Named verifiers with documented training, an assessment route and a technical reviewer who did not perform the engagement. Competence is not a CV — it is a record that somebody else can audit.

4 · Metrology

Measurement traceable to a standard, with calibration certificates and sampling rules stated in the method. A footprint built on an uncalibrated meter is a number, not evidence.

5 · Accreditation

Assessment by a national accreditation body against ISO/IEC 17065 (product certification) and ISO 14065 (GHG validation and verification). This is the longest and most expensive condition, and the one that makes a statement admissible in procurement.

6 · Liability

Professional indemnity insurance and a stated cap, so that a wrong statement has a consequence somewhere other than the client's reputation.
