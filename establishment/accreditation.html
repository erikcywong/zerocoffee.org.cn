Accreditation

Accreditation is an assessment of the organisation, not of a product. It takes a year, it can fail, and nothing we say before it survives is usable for compliance.

Accreditation is an option, not the plan for 2026. Until a signature is required, revenue comes from the evidence layer — measurement, grading and the register — which does not depend on accreditation. The Q3 2027 target stays open as an option; the two-track plan explains what is signed by a partner in the meantime.

What changes when it arrives

  • Statements can be cited in regulated and procurement contexts.
  • The logo may be used with the accreditation mark and its registration number.
  • Annual surveillance becomes compulsory — accreditation is a relationship, not a purchase.
  • The scope of accreditation is public and narrow: what is not listed is not accredited.

What we do not claim today
