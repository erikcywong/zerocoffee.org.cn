Establishment file · Two-track plan

Two tracks: sign through a partner, restructure the body

The question is not whether to become a certification body. It is what to sell in the next twelve months while the structure is being built. Two tracks run in parallel: a licensed signing partner for cash flow now, and a structural rebuild for the right to sign later.

Why this order. Accreditation is a 2027 option, not a 2026 plan. A body that waits for it has no revenue in between, and a body that issues conclusions before it holds accreditation creates the one thing procurement teams check first. The signing layer can be borrowed; the evidence layer cannot.

The three options

OptionWhat it meansCost / lead time Why not
A · SplitHong Kong company limited by guarantee: founder funds it but holds no shares; a majority-independent board with a veto; consulting ban; single-client revenue capped and disclosed ≈ HK$10–20k to establish + annual audit; 2–3 months Correct end state, but nobody pays for it in the first year and control is surrendered up front
B · AllianceDo not sign as a body. Sign an evidence-supply agreement with an already accredited body — it signs, we supply metering, ledger, grading and the register, revenue is shared 1–3 months; near-zero marginal cost Only the share, not the signature premium — but it is revenue now
C · Two tracks ✓B for cash flow, A for structure: sign through a partner today and rebuild the body over 6–12 monthsB now; A over 6–12 months Two narratives to keep straight — this page is how they are kept straight

Nothing in track B is a claim about us. A partner with ISO/IEC 17065 accreditation is the one whose name a buyer can check, and our evidence is what makes its signature cheap to produce and hard to challenge.

Track A — 6 to 12 months

Two acceptable end states, and the choice between them is a business decision rather than a compliance one:

End stateWhen it is rightWhat it costs
Real CLG — a guarantee company with a majority-independent board and a veto A signature is worth more than the cash it costs to be allowed to give one Establishment cost, annual audit, and the loss of unilateral control
Methodology & registry brand — we publish the method and keep the register, and never present ourselves as a certification bodyThe evidence layer is the business, and a signature is a rented serviceAlmost nothing, but the word "certify" leaves the vocabulary

This site currently follows the second end state: methodology and public registry, with a standing statement that it does not issue certification conclusions.
