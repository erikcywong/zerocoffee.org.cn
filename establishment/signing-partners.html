Signing partners

Track B of the two-track plan rents the signature. This page describes the agreement we offer to an accredited body, what each side does under it, and how the countersignature appears on a record.

Status: no partner is contracted. Nothing on this site is a claim that an agreement exists. Until the first evidence-supply agreement is signed, no conclusion is issued under it, and every call to action that needs a signature says so.

What the agreement covers

TurnWe doThe partner does
MeasurementMetered data, hourly emission factors, boundary and functional unitReviews the method and the sampling plan
LedgerBatch ledger, hash chain, revocation, public recompute pageRelies on the ledger as the evidence for its conclusion
GradingA / B / C by metered shareAccepts or rejects the grade under its own procedure
ConclusionPrepares the case fileIssues the statement or label under its accreditation
RegisterWrites the record and the countersignature referenceIs named on the record as the signing body

What the partner gets

BenefitWhy it matters to an accredited body
Cases arrive with the evidence doneA verification that starts at the review, not at the farm
Fewer person-days per conclusionThe expensive part of a small-holder engagement is assembling evidence, not judging it
A public register to point atBuyers can recompute a record without contacting either party
No integration projectThe register is ours; the partner keeps its own systems

What we ask

AskWhy
A named reviewer and a response windowA countersignature that takes a month is not useful to a shipment
The right to refuse without explanationIf the partner cannot refuse, the signature is not independent
Publication of the countersignature referenceThe register must show who signed, not just that something was signed
No fee from the supplier to the partner for a favourable outcomeThe report's own rule: the payer must not be the beneficiary

How the record looks once signed

FieldValue
ConclusionSigned by the partner's authorised reviewer
EvidenceHashed by us, recomputable by anyone
Signing bodyThe partner, named in full with its accreditation scope
Our roleMethodology, metering, grading and the register — never the conclusion

How a partner starts

#StepOutput
1Read the method and run one batch against itA case the partner's reviewer can mark up
2Agree the sampling plan and the grade thresholdsA written procedure for this class of engagement
3Sign the evidence-supply agreementTwo named signatories, one scope, one fee split
4Countersign the first labelThe record appears in the register with the partner named

Until step 3 is done, the honest answer to "who signs" is: nobody. That is what this page exists to say.

Related: the two-track plan places this agreement on track B; the fee schedule publishes the buyer-side table and the signature share.
