Why publish this at all

Because the alternative is a logo and an assertion. Buyers increasingly ask for the standards a body works to and the accreditation it holds; when the answer is “not yet”, saying it plainly is cheaper than being found out during evaluation.

Every page of this site carries the same line in the header: accreditation in progress — not yet accredited. It is not decorative. Until the national body has assessed us, our statements are professional opinions, not accredited conformity assessment.

What this file is not

  • Not an accreditation certificate. The certificate is issued by HKAS, not by us.
  • Not a quality mark. We assess carbon claims; we do not grade produce.
  • Not a legal opinion. Liability, scope and exclusions are in the engagement letter.
