Method · ZCV-SPEC-001

CJV-MTH-1.0 — the specification

The method this registry runs on, published as a numbered document, so that a reviewer can check a claim against a version rather than against a website. Version 1.0, 2026-11-06, CC BY 4.0.

Author: Wong, Erik C. · ORCID 0009-0004-6360-4415 · ZeroCarbon Verify (HK) Limited (in incorporation) · contact erik.cywong@live.com

What this document is. A normative specification: the verified unit, the evidence grade, the counterfactual baseline rule, four refusal classes, the hash chain and the claim partition around countersignature. It is not an accredited standard and confers no certification. Section 15 of the document states the seven limitations, including the author's dual role.

The five properties of a verified unit

PropertyRequirementFails when
MeteredThe footprint derives from measured quantitiesOnly invoices exist
BoundedBoundary and functional unit fixed before data collectionChosen after the result
AttributedOne owner, one location, one batch idSplit across sites without a rule
CommittedHashed at issuance, not editable afterwardsA value is revised silently
RecomputableA third party reproduces the hash without the issuerVerification requires asking us

A record failing one property is not a weaker unit. It is not a unit.

Verify the register yourself

Download the two files above and run the verifier. It needs no dependencies beyond Python 3.8 and no network access; it recomputes every hash from the published fields.

python verify_register.py

  file:            register-labels.json
  records:         20
  declared head:   54980f3d
  recomputed head: 54980f3d
  method:          CJV-MTH-1.0 / 9b12abef

  OK — payload, chain, arithmetic and grades all verify

Change one digit in the snapshot and the run fails, naming the first record that breaks and every record after it. That is the whole point: a claim that cannot be checked by someone who does not trust us is not evidence.

What it is not

Not thisWhy
An accredited standardNo accreditation body has assessed this body; conformance is a statement about structure, not competence
A certificationThe specification defines records; conclusions are countersigned by a licensed partner
Evidence of effectivenessIt contains no pilot and no evaluation — Section 15 says so
A cryptographic guaranteeFNV-1a resists casual modification, not an adversary who rewrites the whole chain
