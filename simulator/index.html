Where the money comes from

Year 1 is a verification year: first-time assessment dominates. From year 2 the recurring base is surveillance plus labels, which is why the margin improves without new sales.

What the buyer pays separately

Model, not forecast. Person-days are fixed per engagement: 6 for the first-year verification, 2 for surveillance, 0.5 for administration, plus a 5% label sample at 0.08 day each; day rate ¥3,800 plus 25% overhead. No customer has bought this yet — the ramp is an assumption, the arithmetic is not.
