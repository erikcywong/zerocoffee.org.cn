What we sell

Three products the evidence layer can sell today

None of them needs accreditation, because none of them is a certification conclusion. They are what makes a licensed partner's signature cheap to produce and hard to challenge.

Standard

The verified unit

One batch, one boundary, one ledger entry, one recomputable hash. Metred, bounded, attributed, committed, recomputable. Buyer pays ¥40–80 per batch; the grower pays nothing.

What we do

Three things, kept strictly apart

Verification, assurance and advisory are different services with different liability. We only sell the first two, because selling the third to the same client invalidates both.

How it works

Four stages, fixed in advance

The stages and the end points are registered before any data is collected. That is what makes the result auditable rather than arguable.

01 Engagement & scopeApplication, boundary, functional unit, data availability. Free of charge; no commitment. → 02 Document reviewMethodology conformity, factor provenance, allocation logic. Three working days. → 03 On-site or remote verificationInstrument calibration, sampling, ledger sampling and re-derivation. → 04 Technical review & register entryA reviewer independent of the measurement team completes the review. Insights

Published method, open to challenge

Everything we rely on is written down with a version number and a challenge window. If a rule is wrong, the way to beat it is to submit a counter-example.

Methodology

Twelve rules, one hash

Thresholds derived from cost asymmetry, two-threshold triage, hard gates, abstention semantics. A method change ships a new version; old labels keep pointing at the old one.

Carbon

The same kilowatt is not the same carbon

Marginal grid intensity varies by hour. Scheduling load into the low-carbon window is the largest single lever on an aeroponic footprint — and it is measurable from the meter.

Why the payer cannot be the beneficiary
