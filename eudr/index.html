Services · EUDR evidence pack

Container-level EUDR evidence pack

The EU regulation asks the importer to file a due-diligence statement for every shipment. What it does not provide is the evidence underneath that statement. We supply it: plot, batch and container, each step hashed to the one before, recomputable by anyone.

Scope, stated first. We do not decide whether land was deforested, and we do not file the due-diligence statement — that is the EU operator's act. We package and freeze the evidence the operator and its certifier work from. Any conclusion that needs a signature is countersigned by a licensed partner.
