About

An assurance body, structured so that it can say no

ZeroCarbon Verify is being established as a Hong Kong company limited by guarantee. It holds no equity in, and takes no commission from, any platform, equipment vendor or grower it verifies.

Independence

No equity, no commissions

The parties we verify cannot own us, and we cannot be paid by a seller for a favourable conclusion. Board and verifiers declare relationships annually.

Method ownership

The method is ours to publish

Platforms are licensed to compute under CJV-MTH-1.0. They cannot edit it and then describe the result as verified.

Veto

The verifier can refuse

Insufficient metering, unpublished factors, an unco-signed baseline — any one of these produces a qualified opinion or no label, not a softer sentence.

Until accreditation is granted, every statement and label carries “not yet accredited” and may not be used for regulatory compliance. We will not remove that wording to make a page look finished: a buyer who checks the HKAS directory and finds nothing is worse off than one who reads the caveat.
