Where a verified gram of CO₂e changes a decision

We start where the evidence chain is already instrumented. Elsewhere we say no, or we start with a baseline study before promising a label.

Primary focus

Coffee & agriculture

What is verifiable today

Batch electricity from a metered supply, nutrient input mass, packaging, outbound transport, and an embedded-hardware allocation — all with hourly grid factors.

What is not

The conventional-farming baseline. It cannot be measured from your fleet; it must be surveyed, co-signed and frozen before any reduction claim.

Why it matters commercially

A labelled lot can carry primary-data scope 3 into a buyer's own footprint. That is the only reason a premium survives a procurement review.

Food & beverage

Scope 3 primary data

Ingredient-level footprints where the supplier is small and the buyer needs auditable primary data rather than a spend-based estimate.

Manufacturing

Process and embedded emissions

Line-level energy and material balance, allocation across product families, and the embedded carbon of equipment amortised per unit.

Finance & insurance

Evidence packs

Lending and underwriting decisions need a defensible evidence chain, not a sustainability report. We produce the former.

We do not take engagements we cannot finish. If the data source is a spreadsheet with no metering behind it, a verification would be a review of the spreadsheet — which is worth something, but must be sold and described as a review, not a verified footprint.

Have a lot and a buyer?

Send the batch shape and the claim. We will tell you within two working days whether it is verifiable.
