The verified unit

A carbon claim is only as good as the smallest thing anyone can check. This page defines that thing: the verified unit — one batch, one boundary, one ledger entry, one recomputable hash.

What a unit is for. It is the unit our evidence layer sells. A buyer who needs primary scope-3 data pays per unit; the grower pays nothing. It is not a credit, not an offset, and not a certificate.

What makes a unit verifiable

PropertyWhat it means in practiceFails when
MeteredThe number comes from a meter, not from a spend-based estimateOnly invoices exist for the energy
BoundedBoundary and functional unit are fixed before the data arrivesThe boundary is chosen after seeing the result
AttributedOne owner, one location, one batch id — no allocation by proportionA batch is split across sites without a rule
CommittedThe record is hashed into the chain at issuance and cannot be edited afterwardsThe number is revised silently
RecomputableA third party reproduces the hash from published fields, without our serverVerification requires asking us

A unit that fails any one of these is not a weaker unit. It is not a unit.

What a unit carries

FieldMeaning
unit_idBatch identifier, immutable once issued
boundaryCradle-to-gate or whichever boundary the method fixes
functional_unitkgCO₂e per plantlet, per kg, per unit of output
metered_shareShare of the footprint from primary data; decides the grade
gradeA / B / C, set by the metered share, not by the customer
hash, prev_hashFNV-1a over canonical fields plus the previous record
methodMethod id and version the number was produced under

Grades

GradeMetered shareWhat it buys
A≥ 70%Primary scope-3 line item, admissible in most supplier programmes
B≥ 45%Usable with a stated share of secondary factors
C< 45%Directional; discloses how much is estimated

The grade is a statement about data, not about the organisation. A C-grade unit from a well-run site is still a C; an A-grade unit from a site we have never met requires the metering to prove itself first.

Who pays

PartyPaysWhy

The growerNothing for the unitThe measurement is a by-product of running the site; charging the supplier builds the wrong incentive

What a unit is not

NotWhy it matters
A carbon credit or offsetNothing is traded, retired or claimed against another party's emissions
A certificationNo accreditation body has certified this body; conclusions are countersigned by a partner
Comparable across methodsA unit is comparable only inside the method version that produced it
