Evidence product

Container-level EUDR evidence pack

The EU regulation asks the importer to file a due-diligence statement for every shipment. It does not supply the evidence underneath that statement. We do: plot, batch and container, each hashed to the one before, recomputable in a browser — and no compliance conclusion is claimed on our side.

What it contains

Plot coordinates and the batch ledger digest, the footprint label of the batch, the review record, and the field mapping an importer needs when filing.

Price

¥3,000–8,000 per container, or 0.2–0.5% of cargo value. Per container, because enforcement and clearance act on the shipment.
