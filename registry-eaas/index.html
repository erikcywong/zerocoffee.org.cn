Service · Registry as a service

Registry as a service, white label

Every accredited body, industry association and ERP vendor needs a register it can point a buyer at. We run that register under their brand, on the same hash chain we publish our own on.

Why this exists. The signature premium belongs to whoever holds accreditation. The register underneath it does not need accreditation — it needs to be recomputable. That is the layer we sell.

Who buys it

BuyerWhat it lacks todayWhat it gets

Industry associationsA credible place to publish member claimsA sector register with evidence grades and revocation
Large buyersSupplier data it can audit without an engagement per supplierRead access to units its suppliers already issued
ERP and agri-platform vendorsNo evidence layer behind the numbers they already holdAn API that turns their records into recomputable units

What we do not do

Not thisWhy
Sign the tenant's conclusionsThe tenant's accreditation is what makes its signature worth something
Lend our name as a certifierWe hold no accreditation and claim none
Certify the tenant's dataWe run the register; the tenant remains responsible for what it issues
Guarantee the tenant's reputationA register makes conduct visible, it does not make it good
